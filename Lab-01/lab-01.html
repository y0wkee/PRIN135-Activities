Simple Cake Recipe

In this page, you will learn how to bake a cake.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup White Sugar
  • 1/2 cup Softened Unsalted Butter
  • 1 1/2 cups All-purpose Flour
  • 1 3/4 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1/4 tsp Table Salt
  • 2 teaspoons Vanilla Extract
  • 2 large Eggs
  • 1/2 cup Whole Milk

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs and sugar together for one minute until pale and creamy.
  2. Pour in the butter and milk, then mix well.
  3. Sift in the flour, baking powder, and baking soda.
  4. Add the vanilla extract and gently stir until smooth.
  5. Pour the batter into a greased 8-inch cake pan lined with parchment paper.
  6. Bake at 175°C (350°F) for 30–40 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean. Let the cake cool completely before slicing or frosting.
