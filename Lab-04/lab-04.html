The Chronicles of Class of '09

Class of '09 is a dark comedy visual novel game series created by SBN3 that follows Nicole, a manipulative and sociopathic teenage girl, and her friend Jecka navigating the worst aspects of American high school life in Virginia during the late 2000s.

Class of '09

This is the anti-visual novel. Instead of playing as some guy magically able to score with beautiful anime girls, YOU play the anime girl and create mass chaos in the lives of your potential "suitors". Earnest comedy, social accuracy, and late 2000's aesthetic await you in Class of '09!

Class of '09: The Re-Up

The REJECTION SIM returns! Explore a slice of chaotic life in late 2000's American high school as you assume the role of Nicole once again. A 100% fully voiced VN presenting as the most culturally accurate period piece in the history of gaming.

Class of '09: The Flip Side

The final chapter of the Class of '09 VN series... this time from a different perspective! Join Nicole's best friend Jecka as you help her navigate the pitfalls of post-grad life! See what 2009 was truly like in this fully voiced interactive sitcom!
